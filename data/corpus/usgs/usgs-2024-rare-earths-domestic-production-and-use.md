Domestic Production and Use: Rare earths were mined domestically in 2023. Bastnaesite (or bastnäsite), a rareearth fluorocarbonate mineral, was mined as a primary product at a mine in Mountain Pass, CA. Monazite, a
phosphate mineral, was stockpiled as a separated concentrate or included as an accessory mineral in heavy-mineralsand concentrates in the southeastern United States. Mixed rare-earth compounds were also produced in the western
United States. The estimated value of rare-earth compounds and metals imported by the United States in 2023 was
$190 million, a 7% decrease from $208 million in 2022. The estimated leading domestic end use of rare earths was
catalysts. Significant amounts of rare earths are imported as permanent magnets embedded in finished goods. Other
end uses were ceramics and glass, metallurgical applications and alloys, and polishing.
