Recycling: The bulk of secondary antimony is recovered at secondary lead smelters as antimonial lead, most of
which was generated by, and then consumed by, the lead-acid battery industry.
Import Sources (2019–2022): Ore and concentrates: Italy, 36%; China, 35%; India, 15%; Belgium, 9%; and other, 5%.
Oxide: China, 76%; Belgium, 11%; Bolivia, 5%; France, 3%; and other, 5%. Unwrought metal and powder: China,
26%; India, 23%; Vietnam, 12%; Burma, 10%; and other, 29%. Total metal and oxide: China, 63%; Belgium, 8%;
India, 6%; Bolivia, 4%, and other, 19%.
