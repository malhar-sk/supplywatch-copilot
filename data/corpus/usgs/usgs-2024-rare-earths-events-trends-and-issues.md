Events, Trends, and Issues: Global mine production was estimated to have increased to 350,000 tons of REO
equivalent. China’s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology raised 2023 quotas for rare-earth mining and
separation to 240,000 tons and 230,000 tons of REO equivalent, respectively. In 2023, mine production quotas were
allocated to 220,850 tons of light rare earths and 19,150 tons of ion-adsorption clays.
