Recycling: In 2023, cobalt content of purchased scrap represented an estimated 25% of estimated cobalt
consumption.
Import Sources (2019–22): Metal, oxide, and salts: Norway, 25%; Canada, 15%; Finland, 13%; Japan, 12%; and
other, 35%.
